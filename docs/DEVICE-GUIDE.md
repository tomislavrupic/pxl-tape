# PXL Tape device guide

## Sound

Choose an engine, play the keys and record onto tape. Tone/Shape are primary controls, Motion/Colour change each engine’s structure. Expand Envelope for ADSR/play mode, Sample for loop regions/reverse, Effect for its algorithm/routing, and Modulation for source/target/rate/depth. SAVE SOUND embeds your sample; OPEN SOUND preserves the current tape. No factory OP-1 data is used.

Analog, FM, Organ and Pluck remain joined by Cluster (seven detuned oscillators), Digital (ring/fold/quantize), Pulse (cross-modulated pulse trains), Phase (breakpoint distortion), Wave (spectral partials), Dual Envelope (dual oscillators/cross modulation), Voltage (feedback/saturation) and DNA (clocked noise). The stereo Sampler plays your imported audio. These are PXL interpretations of synthesis families rather than original OP-1 code.

## Effect devices

Magnet: variable delay with feedback/damping. Lattice: cross-fed multi-tap echoes. Resonant: driven state-variable filtering with envelope follow. Telecom: narrow-band, clock-reduced/quantized telephone colour. Impact: saturated lowpass and dynamic punch. Coil: coupled damped ambience. Sideband: quadrature-modulated feedback delay. A controls time/frequency; B feedback/resonance; C colour; Mix blends wet and dry. Source prints to tape, Master shapes playback/export. Legacy Drive/Echo/Space follow the recorded tape mix.

## Rhythm

D-Box uses generated kick/snare/hats/claps with pitch, decay, colour and cross modulation. Slice mode divides your loaded sample into eight equal pads. Load audio on Sound; return to Drums and choose Your Sample. Empty or tiny slices remain silent safely.

## Sequences

Enable Melody, press Play. Pattern uses the selected16-step bank. Endless traverses up to128 steps. Finger keys C3–G3 choose phrases. Tombola uses particle motion/gravity to choose notes from the selected bank. Sketch lets you draw the pitch curve. Arpeggio uses held keyboard/MIDI chords, direction and octave span. Swing and gate control timing. Banks expose all128 steps; shift-click melody steps to raise notes, Fill enables a bank, Clear Bank erases its triggers. Sequence data is shared between modes.

## Limits

Tape remains four stereo60-second tracks. Sampling is30 seconds. This release expands the device family; physical radio/sensors, exact original algorithms, multi-slot sound architecture, extended tape capacity and an independent album recorder are not implemented. Use Mix WAV for a final loop and Stems for DAW finishing. Preserve local project files before closing the tab.
