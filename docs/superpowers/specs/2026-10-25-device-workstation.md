# PXL device workstation

User decision: original samples, sounds and algorithms; reproduce the range of devices/effects and creative workflows in PXL style. Exact OP-1 audio or firmware emulation is not required. Native AU/VST3/standalone and browser use the same owned C++ DSP.

Extend the five existing engines with Cluster, Digital, Pulse, Phase, Wave, Dual Envelope, Voltage and DNA synthesis families. Preserve legacy sound IDs. Add selectable seven-device effects (Delay, Grid, resonant filter, Phone, Punch, Spring and frequency-shift delay), source modulation, full ADSR, D-Box controls, drum sample slices, sampler regions/loop/reverse, six sequencers, mixer EQ and compression. Keep original PXL algorithms and generated presets; no factory samples or firmware.

Persist every new control and expanded sequence in native/browser projects, loading older projects with defaults. Controls must be reachable and labeled in both UIs. Named devices must render distinct output and respond to their controls. Keep the tape-first five-page layout and light/dark themes. Preserve previous archives and the live release until the new build passes verification. Existing 60-second stereo tape is the capacity of this release; this is a device expansion, not an exact hardware/storage clone.
